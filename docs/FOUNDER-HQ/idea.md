# FOUNDER OS

## 2031 Nigeria Return & Venture-Building Master Plan

**Planning Horizon:** 2026–2031
**Mission:** Return to Nigeria with the capital, technology, people and operating infrastructure required to build enduring African companies.
**Initial Geographic Focus:** South-East Nigeria + Lagos
**Long-Term Reach:** Nigeria → Africa → Global
**Planning Currency:** Nigerian Naira, with USD reserves for currency protection and international expenditure.

---

# 1. THE MISSION

The objective is not to return to Nigeria in five years looking for something to do.

The objective is to arrive with:

* capital
* intellectual property
* operating products
* international relationships
* technical infrastructure
* a portfolio of validated ventures
* an experienced founder
* a clear operating system

and then construct a physical institution capable of repeatedly building companies.

The ultimate ambition is:

# BUILD COMPANIES THAT MAKE AFRICA MORE CAPABLE.

Some will be commercial.

Some technological.

Some cultural.

Some deeply Igbo.

Some pan-African.

Some global.

But they should share infrastructure, talent, capital and knowledge.

---

# 2. THE STRUCTURE

Do not place every idea inside one operating company.

Create a holding-company structure.

Working concept:

# FOUNDER HOLDCO

The HoldCo owns or controls stakes in separate ventures.

Underneath it could eventually sit:

**Nkwado**
Trade infrastructure.

**Nkwado Reach / Reach**
Physical + digital market-access infrastructure.

**Chinedu AI**
Igbo artificial intelligence, language, knowledge and cultural infrastructure.

**WatchGuard AI**
Preserve as a dedicated venture track based on its existing product thesis rather than forcing it into another company.

**Ogayi**
Preserve as its own venture track and develop according to its existing specification.

**D8N**
Dating/social technology portfolio where it remains strategically viable.

**Weloid**
Software engineering, forensic engineering and technical services.

**Pathfinder**
Life/career pathway infrastructure if validation supports it.

And future ventures that do not exist yet.

The HoldCo becomes the permanent institution.

Individual products can succeed, fail, raise outside investment or be sold without destroying the rest of the ecosystem.

# 37. THE CAPITAL TARGET

I would therefore establish three return thresholds.

## MINIMUM RETURN

# ₦2bn liquid/investable capital

Enough to establish the institution, but requires disciplined sequencing and external capital for major expansion.

## STRONG RETURN

# ₦5bn

Enough to execute approximately the serious Year-One plan described here.

## FORTRESS RETURN

# ₦10bn+

This is the target I would prefer.

Not because ₦10bn should be spent immediately.

Because having it means the founder can make long-term decisions without one failed venture destroying the institution.

The objective for 2031 should therefore be:

# ARRIVE CONTROLLING ACCESS TO ₦5–₦10 BILLION OF CAPITAL.

* founder liquid capital
* retained company earnings
* committed investors
* credit facilities
* strategic partners
* trade-finance facilities

But founder-controlled capital should form a meaningful base.
