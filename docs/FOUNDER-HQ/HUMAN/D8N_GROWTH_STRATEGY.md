# 1. Social Content & Dating Debates

**Goal:** Attention, comments, shares and cultural relevance.

Date9ja should become part of Nigeria's dating conversation rather than behaving like an advertising account.

## Nigerian Men

* Nigerian men are the best men to date. Argue with us. 🇳🇬😂
* Nigerian men make better husbands than British and American men. We said what we said. 🏃🏾‍♂️
* What's one thing Nigerian men actually do WELL in relationships?
* Women who have dated Nigerian men: what surprised you?
* Would you date a Nigerian man?
* What's the biggest misconception about Nigerian men?
* Nigerian men abroad vs Nigerian men at home: is there actually a difference?

## Nigerian Women

* Nigerian women make elite wives. Debate your keyboard. 😂
* What's something Nigerian women do better in relationships?
* Men who have dated Nigerian women: what did you learn?
* Would you marry a Nigerian woman living in Nigeria if you lived abroad?
* Nigerian women abroad vs at home: what's actually different?

## Money

* Who should pay for the first date?
* Should bills be 50/50 in marriage?
* Would you date someone earning significantly less than you?
* Should a woman know her boyfriend's salary?
* Would you marry for financial stability rather than love?
* Is being broke a legitimate reason not to date?

## Marriage

* How long should people date before marriage?
* Would you marry without your parents' approval?
* What's an acceptable age to get married?
* Is marriage still worth it?
* Would you marry someone you've only known for six months?

## Tribe

* Would you marry outside your tribe?
* Which Nigerian tribes date each other most easily?
* Should tribe matter in 2026?
* What's the funniest thing your parents have said about marrying outside your tribe?

Never turn ethnic discussion into degrading stereotypes or hostility.

## Diaspora

* Would you marry someone currently living in Nigeria if you lived abroad?
* Would you relocate for love?
* Would you maintain a relationship across continents for one year?
* Why do Nigerians move thousands of kilometres away and still marry Nigerians? 😂
* Diaspora Nigerians: home or abroad—which dating pool is better?

## Red Flags

Recurring:

**DATE9JA RED FLAG OF THE WEEK 🚩**

## Green Flags

Recurring:

**DATE9JA GREEN FLAG OF THE WEEK 💚**

## Date9ja Court

Present a dating dispute.

Followers become the jury.

**DATE9JA COURT ⚖️**
